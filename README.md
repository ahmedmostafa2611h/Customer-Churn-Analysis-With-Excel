# 📊 Customer Churn Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

Customer churn is one of the most critical business challenges for subscription-based companies. Retaining existing customers is often more cost-effective than acquiring new ones.

This project analyzes customer churn data to identify the primary factors influencing customer attrition and provides actionable business insights through an interactive Excel dashboard.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

The company is experiencing customer loss but lacks visibility into:

- Why customers are leaving.
- Which customer segments are most likely to churn.
- The impact of competitors on customer retention.
- Which customers should be targeted with retention strategies.

This dashboard transforms raw customer data into meaningful insights that support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 📈 Dashboard KPIs

- **Total Customers:** 6,687
- **Churned Customers:** 1,796
- **Churn Rate:** 26.86%
- **Average Monthly Charge:** $31.03
- **Average Revenue per User (ARPU):** $1084

---

## 🔍 Dashboard Analysis

### 1. Customer Churn Overview
Provides a high-level summary of customer churn and key business metrics.

### 2. Competitor Churn Analysis
Identifies the percentage of customers who left because competitors offered:

- Better devices
- Better pricing
- Faster download speeds
- More data

### 3. Churn Reasons
Breaks down the primary reasons behind customer churn to highlight the most impactful business issues.

### 4. Demographic Analysis
Analyzes churn across different customer demographics to identify high-risk customer groups.

### 5. Age Group Analysis
Compares the number of customers and churn rate across different age segments.

### 6. Consumption Analysis
Examines the relationship between monthly data consumption and customer churn behavior.

---

## 💡 Key Insights

- Customer churn reached **26.86%**, indicating a significant retention challenge.
- Competitor offers and better devices are the leading causes of customer churn.
- Churn behavior varies across different age groups.
- Customers with lower data usage exhibit different churn patterns, suggesting opportunities for personalized retention campaigns.

---

## 📊 Business Recommendations

- Introduce personalized retention offers before customers switch to competitors.
- Launch device upgrade programs for loyal customers.
- Develop targeted marketing campaigns for high-risk customer segments.
- Improve customer engagement for low-usage customers.
- Continuously monitor churn trends using interactive dashboards.
